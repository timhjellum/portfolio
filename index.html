Underground Films

Underground Films was still a concept when I joined. I was responsible for shaping that vision into a cohesive digital experience spanning a responsive website, mobile apps, and streaming integrations with Roku, Prime Video, and Netflix. Through persona exercises and user stories, I defined audience needs and translated those insights into sitemaps, user flows, wireframes, and interactive prototypes. I also studied competing platforms to identify familiar interaction patterns, using established conventions to make the new service intuitive to navigate.

An Invitation to Explore

I designed the landing page to spark curiosity from the first visit. Animated film posters introduce the catalog, while a clear invitation to sign in or create a free account gives visitors a natural next step.

02

A Personal Starting Point

The member homepage balances discovery with familiarity. A visual slider highlights upcoming releases, featured titles, and popular films, while recommendations based on viewing habits make the experience more personal.

03

Built for Discovery

For a film-savvy audience, finding the right title matters as much as discovering something unexpected. I focused on making a diverse catalog of independent and international films easy to explore across genres, languages, and countries.
