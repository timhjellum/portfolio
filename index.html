Case study · 01

Underground Films

Underground Films was still a concept when I joined. I was responsible for shaping that vision into a cohesive digital experience spanning a responsive website, mobile apps, and streaming integrations with Roku, Prime Video, and Netflix. Through persona exercises and user stories, I defined audience needs and translated those insights into sitemaps, user flows, wireframes, and interactive prototypes. I also studied competing platforms to identify familiar interaction patterns, using established conventions to make the new service intuitive to navigate.
